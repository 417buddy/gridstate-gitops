GridState Project 4 — k9s Drift Observation

Cluster context:
k3d-gridstate-region-a

Namespace:
gridstate

Observed resource:
Deployment/gridstate-api

Baseline:
gridstate-api was running with 2 desired, 2 ready, and 2 available replicas.

Deliberate drift:
The deployment was manually scaled directly against the live cluster:

kubectl scale deployment gridstate-api -n gridstate --replicas=5

Observation:
The deployment entered a drifted state with desired replicas set to 5.
The immediate kubectl observation showed 2/5 while additional replicas were being reconciled.

Auto-remediation:
ArgoCD selfHeal restored the deployment specification to the Git-declared value of 2 replicas.

Final k9s observation:
gridstate-api      2/2 ready
gridstate-worker   2/2 ready
redis              1/1 ready

Measured correction time:
64291 ms

Conclusion:
k9s was useful for quickly observing workload-level state and the final restoration of the deployment without leaving the terminal.
