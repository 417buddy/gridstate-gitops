GridState Project 5 — Recovery Targets
========================================

Drill objective:
Validate full Region A evacuation and recovery into Region B
with measurable Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point
Objective (RPO).

RTO TARGET
----------
Target: <= 10 minutes

Definition:
Elapsed time from confirmed Region A failure (T0) until Region B
successfully accepts and processes a real validation workload (T1).

RPO TARGET
----------
Target: <= 5 minutes

Definition:
Maximum acceptable amount of GridState state/workload that may be
lost between the latest successful verified backup timestamp and
the confirmed Region A failure timestamp.

PRE-DRILL REQUIREMENTS
----------------------
1. Region A healthy.
2. Region B healthy.
3. Region B Redis persistent.
4. Region A Redis persistent.
5. Velero installed and operational.
6. Backup Storage Location verified.
7. At least one successful verified backup.
8. At least one successful restore test.
9. Uptime Kuma available for timestamped monitoring.
10. Chaos experiment must not begin until backup/restore validation passes.

RTO/RPO measurement will use actual timestamps recorded during the
drill. No estimated or simulated values will be used.
