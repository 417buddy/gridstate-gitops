GridState Project 5 — Recovery Targets

Date established: 2026-09-01

RTO TARGET
Maximum acceptable recovery time: 10 minutes

Definition:
Time from confirmed Region A failure to successful
GridState job processing in Region B.

RPO TARGET
Maximum acceptable data loss window: 1 minute

Definition:
Maximum amount of successfully committed GridState
state that may be lost following Region A failure.

IMPORTANT:
These targets were established BEFORE the Region A
chaos experiment and must not be changed based on the
eventual drill outcome.
