GRIDSTATE PROJECT 5 — RECOVERY TARGETS

Project:
DevOps Project 5 — Full Region Evacuation Drill

Product:
GridState

Target Date:
2026-09-01

PRIMARY REGION:
region-a

FAILOVER REGION:
region-b

RTO TARGET:
10 minutes

Definition:
Maximum acceptable elapsed time from confirmed region-a
failure to region-b successfully accepting and processing
GridState jobs.

RPO TARGET:
5 minutes

Definition:
Maximum acceptable amount of committed GridState state/jobs
that may be lost between the latest successful Velero backup
and the region-a failure.

RECOVERY OBJECTIVE:
Region-b must operate GridState independently of region-a.

BACKUP OBJECTIVE:
Redis state must be captured by Velero and subsequently
restored and verified.

IMPORTANT:
These recovery targets are established BEFORE the Project 5
chaos experiment. They must not be changed after observing
the drill results.

RTO MEASUREMENT:
T0 = confirmed region-a service failure
T1 = region-b successfully accepting/processing jobs

RTO = T1 - T0

RPO MEASUREMENT:
Tbackup = timestamp of latest successful backup containing
the verified Redis/application state

Tfailure = timestamp of region-a failure

RPO = Tfailure - Tbackup
